A CHARTER ORDINANCE AMENDING CHARTER ORDINANCE NO. 5 OF THE CITY OF DE SOTO RELATED TO APPOINTMENT OF CITY OFFICERS BY ELIMINATING THE APPOINTMENT OF THE CITY CLERK BY THE MAYOR AND CITY COUNCIL AND PROVIDING SAID POSITION AND THE MUNICIPAL COURT CLERK POSITION AS EMPLOYEES RESPONSIBLE TO THE CITY ADMINISTRATOR.

(Repealed by C.O. No. 23)
