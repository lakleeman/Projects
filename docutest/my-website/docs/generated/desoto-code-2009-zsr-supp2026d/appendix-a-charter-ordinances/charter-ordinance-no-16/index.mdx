A CHARTER ORDINANCE REPEALING CITY OF DE SOTO CHARTER ORDINANCE NO. 9, WHICH EXEMPTED THE CITY FROM K.S.A. 79-5021 THROUGH K.S.A. 79-5036 REGARDING AGGREGATE TAX LEVY LIMITATIONS

WHEREAS, Charter Ordinance No. 9 exempted the City of De Soto from K.S.A. 79-5021 through K.S.A. 79-5036, which statutes placed certain limitations upon taxing subdivisions; and

WHEREAS, K.S.A. 79-5021 through K.S.A. 79-5036 expired on July 1, 1999 pursuant to K.S.A. 79-5038 and, therefore, the self-imposed limitations in Charter Ordinance No. 9 are no longer applicable.

NOW THEREFORE, BE IT ORDAINED BY THE GOVERNING BODY OF THE CITY OF DE SOTO, KANSAS:

Section 1. The City of De Soto, Kansas, by virtue of the power vested in it by Article 12, Section 5 of the Constitution of the State of Kansas; hereby repeals City of De Soto, Kansas Charter Ordinance No. 9.

(7-19-2001)
