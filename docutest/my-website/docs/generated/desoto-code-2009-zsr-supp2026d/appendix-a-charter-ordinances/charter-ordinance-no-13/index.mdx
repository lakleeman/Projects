A CHARTER ORDINANCE REPEALING CITY OF DE SOTO, KANSAS, CHARTER ORDINANCE NO. 8, WHICH EXEMPTED THE CITY FROM PROVISIONS OF K. S .A. 19-3623(f) RELATING TO DETACHMENT OF RURAL FIRE DISTRICT TERRITORY.

Section 1: The City of De Soto, Kansas, by virtue of the power vested in it by Article 12, Section 5 of the Constitution of the State of Kansas; hereby repeals City of De Soto, Kansas, Charter Ordinance No. 8.

(2-12-99)
