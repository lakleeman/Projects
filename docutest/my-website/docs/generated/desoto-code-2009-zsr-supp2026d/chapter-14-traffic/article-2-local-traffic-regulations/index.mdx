## 14-201. Traffic control devices and markings.

The Standard Traffic Ordinance as adopted is hereby modified by adding thereto the following:

The governing body may, by resolution, establish and fix the location of such traffic control devices as may be deemed necessary to guide and warn traffic under the provisions of this chapter, other traffic ordinances and the state laws. The city shall place and maintain such traffic control signs, signals and devices when and as may be required by the authority of the governing body to make effective the provisions of this chapter and other ordinances for the regulation of traffic.

(Code 2009)

## 14-203. Load restrictions upon vehicles using certain streets.

(a) When signs are erected giving notice thereof, no person shall operate any truck with a gross weight in excess of the amounts specified herein at any time upon any of the following streets, bridges, or parts of streets or bridges within the city:

(1) 20,000 pound limit.

Wea Street

Peoria Street

Wyandotte Street from 83rd Street to 84th Street

Osage Street from 83rd Street to 84th Street

Jaycee Street

(2) 53,999 pound limit.

Ottawa Street from 79th Street to 83rd Street

89th Street from and between the intersection of Waverly Road and Kill Creek Road.

(3) Wyandotte Bridge [a/k/a Bridge SH-64 or NBI Bridge #000000000460240].

Any truck exceeding the following limits is prohibited: (a) Truck Type 3 = 22 Tons; (b) Truck Type 3S2 = 31 Tons; and Truck Type 3-3 = 44 Tons.

(b) Exceptions. Exceptions to this shall apply only for necessary ingress and egress for residents on the specific area of restriction and except for deliveries by truck as defined herein to a place of business and/or residence which exists on the area of restriction. All other truck travel along the area of restriction is strictly prohibited.

(c) Determination of Gross Weight. For purposes of this article, gross weight shall mean the greater of the weight for which the vehicle is licensed pursuant to K.S.A. 8-143(2) and the actual gross weight of the vehicle. Law enforcement officers are authorized under this article to weigh any truck at any grain elevator in the city and within five miles of the point of alleged violation at the expense of the city. It shall be a violation of this article for any owner or driver of such vehicle to refuse when directed by a law enforcement officer to drive to an available weighing facility as directed by such law enforcement officer.

(Ord. 756; Ord. 995; Ord. 2375; Ord. 2658)

## 14-204. Truck traffic; restrictions.

(a) Area of Restriction: There shall be a daily prohibition against the operation of any truck upon Waverly Road from and between the intersection of 83^rd^Street and Explorers Trail in the city.

(b) Daily Prohibition Defined: Daily prohibition as used in this article shall mean that the prohibition shall apply equally to all days of the week.

(c) Truck Defined: A truck for purposes of this article shall include all motor vehicles or combination of vehicles which are included within those vehicles required by the Kansas Secretary of Transportation to be plated, licensed and tagged as a truck and which have a gross registered vehicle weight equal to or greater than 53,999 pounds. Further, this restriction shall apply to all trucks traveling on the area of restriction except only for necessary ingress and egress for residents on the specific area of restriction and except for deliveries by a truck defined herein to place of business and/or residence which exists on the area of restriction. All other truck travel along the area of restriction is strictly prohibited.

(Ord. 1024, Sec. 1; Ord. 2658)

## 14-206. Restriction of parking on unimproved surfaces.

(a) It shall be unlawful for any vehicle owner, property owner, resident or tenant to allow a vehicle to be parked or stored outside of a building on any surface other than an area designated as a driveway or a parking area. A designated driveway or parking area shall be paved or, if not paved, at a minimum shall consist of four (4) inches of compacted gravel base. Nothing herein shall be construed as a waiver of, or otherwise affect, the City’s Zoning Regulations or surfacing standards for new construction.

(b) Exceptions. This section shall not apply to agricultural equipment parked on property used primarily for agricultural purposes; to recreational vehicles, boats or hauling trailers parked in compliance with the provisions of this Code; or to temporary events where parking on unpaved surfaces or areas not designated as a driveway or parking area: (1) is for a duration of no more than 48 hours; or (2) has been specifically approved by the City.

(c) Penalty. The city may file a complaint in the municipal court of the city against any person violating this section and, upon conviction of any violation of the provisions of this section, such person may be fined in an amount not to exceed $100 or be imprisoned not to exceed 30 days or be both fined and imprisoned. Each day during or on which a violation occurs or continues after notice has been served shall constitute an additional or separate offense.

(Ord. 2043, Sec. 1)

## 14-212. Parking Restriction of Large Trucks, Exceptions.

(a) For purposes of this parking restriction, the term “Truck” shall be defined as all motor vehicles or combination of vehicles which are included within those vehicles required by the Kansas Secretary of Transportation to be plated, licensed and tagged as a truck and which have a gross registered vehicle weight equal to or greater than 53,999 pounds.

(b) The Governing Body hereby prohibits the parking of Trucks on any public streets, rights-of-way, and/or other public parking areas which adjoin private property that is zoned for residential use.

(c) The above prohibition shall not apply to:

(1) Emergency situations;

(2) Governmental storage or operations;

(3) Trucks actively engaged in legitimate loading or unloading activities;

(4) Trucks that are part of lawfully permitted construction or a service call actively underway on adjacent property; or

(5) The Truck is making routine deliveries to adjacent property.

(Ord. 2493)
