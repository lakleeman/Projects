## 4-901. International residential code.

As set forth in Section 4-202 of this code, the city has adopted the International Residential Code which contains regulations governing fencing for pools, which regulations are incorporated herein by reference.

(Code 2009)

## 4-902. Violations.

Any person who owns property upon which a pool is maintained in violation of this article shall be deemed to be in violation hereof. Any resident of property upon which a pool is maintained under such person’s control in violation of this article shall be deemed to be in violation hereof. Each day that any such violation continues shall be deemed a separate violation.

(Ord. 717, Sec. 5)
