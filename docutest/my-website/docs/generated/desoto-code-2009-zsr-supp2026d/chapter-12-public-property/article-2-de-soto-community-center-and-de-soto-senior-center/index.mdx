## 12-201. Use fee.

The use fee for the De Soto Community Center or the De Soto Senior Center shall be established by the City’s annual Fee Resolution.

(Ord. 500, Sec. 1; Ord. 1046, Sec. 1; Code 2009)

## 12-202. Same; exception.

Section 12-201 of this article shall not apply to use of the De Soto Senior Center by Services for Seniors, Inc.

(Ord. 500, Sec. 2)

## 12-203. Same; acknowledgment of terms.

Each user of the De Soto Community Center or the Johnson County Nutrition Site, with the exception of Services for Seniors, Inc., shall execute an acknowledgment of the terms of use of the facility as required by the city administrator or his designee, a copy of which acknowledgment is attached hereto and made a part hereof as if it were fully set out herein.

(Ord. 500, Sec. 3; Code 2009)
